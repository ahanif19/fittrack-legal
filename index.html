FitTrack SMS Program

FitTrack is a personal fitness tracking app. Our SMS program lets you text our system to receive automated replies and updates about your fitness tracking activity.

How to Join

Text the keyword START to our program number to opt in. By texting START, you consent to receive recurring automated text messages from FitTrack related to your fitness activity. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for assistance.
