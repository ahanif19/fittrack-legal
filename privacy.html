FitTrack SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

FitTrack ("we", "us", "our") offers an SMS messaging program that lets users text our system to receive replies and updates related to their fitness tracking activity within the FitTrack app.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to FitTrack SMS by texting the keyword START to our number, we collect your mobile phone number and the content of the messages you send to us. We may also associate this information with your FitTrack app account and fitness activity data (such as workout logs and progress summaries) in order to generate relevant replies.

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number and message content solely to operate the FitTrack SMS program, including sending you replies, confirmations, and updates about your fitness activity that you have requested by texting us.

Sharing of Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opting Out

You can opt out of FitTrack SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. After you send STOP, we will send you a final confirmation message and you will no longer receive SMS messages from FitTrack. Reply HELP at any time for assistance, or contact us at ahanif7926@gmail.com.

Message Frequency and Fees

Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at ahanif7926@gmail.com.
